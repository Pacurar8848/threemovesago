Privacy

Short version: no accounts, no ads, no tracking cookies. We keep only what the game needs.

Who is responsible

Three Moves Ago is run by 22 Summers. Contact: mycampus.students@gmail.com.

What is stored on your device

The game saves your progress in your browser’s local storage, not in cookies: the cases you played, your attempts, your streak and stats, your sound and theme settings, and a random player ID. This data stays on your device. You can delete it at any time by clearing this site’s data in your browser.

What we store on our server

When you finish a case, we save one anonymous result so we can show how many players solved each case:

DataWhy
Case numberTo count results per case
Random player IDSo each player is counted once per case
Solved or not, number of attemptsFor the “players solved it” statistics
Time of the resultFor the statistics

The player ID is a random number created in your browser. It is not linked to your name, email or account, and we do not ask for any of these. Results are stored with Supabase, in the EU (Frankfurt, Germany).

Your player ID: none yet. If you want your results deleted, send us this ID by email.

Hosting

The site is hosted by Cloudflare. Like any web host, Cloudflare processes your IP address and basic request data to deliver the pages and protect the site from abuse. We do not use this data to identify or follow you.

What we don’t do

  • No advertising and no ad trackers.
  • No analytics cookies and no fingerprinting.
  • No selling or sharing of data.
  • No third-party fonts or scripts: fonts are served from our own site.

Your rights

Under the GDPR you can ask to see or delete the data linked to your player ID, and you can complain to your data protection authority (in Romania, the ANSPDCP). Write to mycampus.students@gmail.com.
